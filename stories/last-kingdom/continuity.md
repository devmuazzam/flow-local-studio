# The Last Kingdom: War of Blood & Magic — continuity guide

Format: 16:9 landscape; six connected 10-second shots per minute. Photorealistic cinematic fantasy CGI with natural performance, physically weighted movement, detailed skin and armor, atmospheric smoke and volumetric light.

## Fixed characters

**Gromak:** adult male green orc, eight feet tall, heavily muscled, protruding lower tusks, scar over the left brow, shaved scalp with one short black braid, battered black iron armor. His signature weapon is one enormous black iron warhammer held in his right hand. Use the warhammer throughout; the source draft inconsistently alternated axe and hammer. His black armored war beast stays behind the orc line after he dismounts.

**King Aldric:** adult human man, about forty, shoulder-length dark hair, short dark beard, silver armor with gold edging, royal blue cape, one straight sword held in his right hand. His awakened form retains the same face, body, armor silhouette and cape; blue-gold runes light across the armor and two translucent spectral dragon wings appear behind him. He remains human rather than changing into a dragon.

**Seraphina:** adult human woman, long silver hair, blue eyes, dark-blue embroidered robes, one ancient staff with a blue crystal. Her magic is electric blue with restrained white highlights. She occupies the human gate tower until an explicit move is shown.

**The Destroyer:** enormous horned demon, black armored scales, burning red eyes, a massive humanoid silhouette. Blood-red portal light and heavy iron chains identify its arrival. Keep the same horn arrangement and scale from its first reveal onward.

## Battlefield geography

The castle and human gate are screen-right; the orc approach and red portal are screen-left. The battle occupies a broad ruined stone causeway between them. Seraphina's gate tower is above and behind the human defensive line. Stay on the same side of this action axis. Show all travel or turns; do not teleport characters between shots.

## Visual and audio continuity

Maintain a single overcast dusk, cool smoke and muted earth tones. Human magic is blue-gold; hostile portal energy is red. Redness comes from magic, not visible blood. Combat can be intense without gore or graphic wounds.

Repeat this bible, the complete preceding story context and the current action in every generation prompt. Review the actual ending of each generated clip before producing the next. Use the actual preceding ending frame as a starting image where supported; a frame reference is not a deterministic random seed.

One clear dominant action per ten seconds, with a simple emotional reaction. Keep spoken lines short enough to perform naturally within the shot. Never ask the model to replay earlier events when providing history.

Preserve wind, distant battle rumble and armor ambience across shot boundaries. Keep a continuous local ambience bed during assembly; use one separately assembled score if music is desired, rather than six independently generated scores. Retain original generated sound effects. Check for low ambience sections as well as digital silence before export.
